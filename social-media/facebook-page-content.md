## About Section (Long Description)
Welcome to Moderate Populist — a one-person effort to lower the political temperature and remind you: the situation we're in right now is not the end of the world.

**Our Mission:**
We believe most Americans occupy the moderate center, rejecting extremes on both sides and demanding practical solutions. Whether you voted for Trump or Biden, whether you identify as conservative, progressive, libertarian, or independent — if you believe citizens should have more power than corporations and special interests, you belong here.

**What We Do:**
- Present balanced, evidence-based analysis of major issues
- Highlight areas of common ground where most Americans agree
- Provide left AND right perspectives on solutions
- Champion practical reforms over partisan theater
- Encourage real conversations over social media arguments

**Our Values:**
- Defend democratic institutions and checks & balances
- Respect for all Americans regardless of background
- Evidence over ideology
- Real conversations matter more than online arguments

**The 4 Cs of Democratic Engagement:**
Communication • Collaboration • Compromise • Making Your Case

Visit our website to explore issues, find common ground, and learn how you can make a difference in your community.

## Weekly Content Ideas

### Monday - "Common Ground Monday"
Highlight an issue where 70%+ of Americans agree across party lines.

Example post:
> 💊 **Common Ground Monday**
>
> Did you know? 80-90% of Americans — including gun owners — support universal background checks for all firearm purchases.
>
> This isn't a left or right issue. It's a common-sense safety measure most of us agree on.
>
> What other issues do you think most Americans agree on? 👇
>
> #CommonGround #AmericanUnity

---

### Wednesday - "Fact Check Wednesday"
Present verified facts about a controversial topic without partisan spin.

Example post:
> 📊 **Fact Check Wednesday**
>
> Healthcare costs: Americans pay 2-3x more than other developed nations for similar outcomes.
>
> This isn't about left vs. right — it's about getting value for what we pay.
>
> Both sides have proposals. What matters is what actually works.
>
> Read more: [link to healthcare page]
>
> #HealthcareFacts #BeyondPartisanship

---

### Friday - "Conversation Starter Friday"
Pose a thoughtful question to encourage respectful discussion.

Example post:
> 💬 **Conversation Starter Friday**
>
> If you could sit down with someone who votes differently than you, what's ONE question you'd genuinely want to understand about their perspective?
>
> Rules: Be curious, not confrontational. We're here to understand, not to "win."
>
> 👇 Share below
>
> #RealConversations #ListenFirst

## Content Guidelines

1. **Never attack individuals** — critique ideas and policies, not people
2. **Always present both perspectives** — even when you disagree with one
3. **Lead with facts** — opinions are welcome but clearly labeled
4. **Encourage dialogue** — ask questions, don't lecture
5. **Stay calm** — model the behavior you want to see
6. **Highlight agreement** — common ground is more newsworthy than conflict
7. **Be authentic** — this is a one-person effort, and that's okay
